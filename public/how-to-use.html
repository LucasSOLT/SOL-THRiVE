LearnWorlds LMS Walkthrough

Navigating Squads A, B & C and the Review Center

Follow these four operational steps to access your private cohort group, review peer case studies, and submit weekly clinical homework assignments.

Step 01

Sign In to LearnWorlds

Go to thrive-coaching.learnworlds.com and log in using the email address associated with your Practitioner Lab enrollment.

Step 02

Access Your Squad Space

Navigate to Community → 5 to THRiVE Practitioner Lab — Squads and open your assigned breakout space (Squad A: Seats 1–7, Squad B: Seats 8–14, or Squad C: Seats 15–21).

Step 03

Submit to Review Center

Under your active course module, click Review Center Assignment. Export your diagnostic dossier from results.html or profile.html and paste it into the submission text box.

Step 04

Bypass Setup Overlays

If a blank white screen appears at /author/courses due to browser privacy extensions, use the 1-click DevTools bypass script below to unlock the dashboard immediately.

🛠️ DevTools Modal Bypass One-Liner

Removes .wizard-window-wrapper.-school overlay and re-enables body overflow scrolling in 1 click.

📖 Read Troubleshooting Manual ↗ 
(() => {
  const selectors = ['.wizard-window-wrapper', '.wizard-window-wrapper.-school', '.wizard-window', '.wizard-backdrop', '.modal-backdrop', '.lw-modal-backdrop'];
  selectors.forEach(sel => document.querySelectorAll(sel).forEach(el => el.remove()));
  ['overflow', 'overflow-y', 'overflow-x', 'height', 'position'].forEach(prop => {
    document.body.style.removeProperty(prop);
    document.documentElement.style.removeProperty(prop);
  });
  document.body.style.setProperty('overflow', 'auto', 'important');
  document.documentElement.style.setProperty('overflow', 'auto', 'important');
  document.body.classList.remove('modal-open', 'wizard-open', 'no-scroll', 'has-modal', '-locked');
  console.log('[THRiVE LMS]: Setup popup purged. Dashboard unlocked!');
})();
